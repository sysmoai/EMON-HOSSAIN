## Decision

Prepare one AI-solutions discovery hub and three practical solution pages:
1. Customer messaging, handoffs and human-reviewed lead support.
2. Repetitive business workflow automation with deterministic rules and bounded AI.
3. Owner-controlled websites, trustworthy search foundations and verified lead routing.

These are *hypothesized buyer problems*, NOT a ranked Bangladesh keyword list or proven willingness-to-pay report. The main site is an authority and education surface; commercial delivery is considered in the separate SYSmoAI context.

## Demand validation protocol

1. Interview at least 10 consented potential customers across relevant buyer segments before claiming a winning niche.
2. Record process frequency, exceptions, problem severity, tools, decision-maker, procurement constraints and budget.
3. Ask for paid-pilot intent, not just generic interest; don't conflate a like, search or enquiry with a purchase.
4. Select a bounded pilot; record baseline and acceptance criteria before implementing.
5. Log all implementation and continuing costs; calculate operating contribution margin only with actual figures.
6. Publish case studies only when evidence is verified and the person/client authorizes public disclosure.
